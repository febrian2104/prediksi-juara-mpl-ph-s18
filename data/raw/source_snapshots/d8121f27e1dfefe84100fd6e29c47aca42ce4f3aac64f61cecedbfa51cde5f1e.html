MPL PH Season 12 playoffs: Schedule, results, format, where to watch

Which teams will defend the homeland?

By Jules Elona October 28, 2023 4:45 pm
Share Article

Updated on October 29, 8:03 p.m. (GMT+8): Updated scores.

The MPL PH Season 12 playoffs will see the grand finalists secure a slot at the M5 World Championship, which will be held in the Philippines later this year.

Furthermore, the top four teams will secure a slot at the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Professional League Invitational 2023 (MPLI 2023), held in Indonesia in November.

M4 world champions, ECHO, secured the top spot in the regular season with an impressive 11-3 record. Following closely in the standings are IESF 2023 champions, AP Bren, who finished with a 7-7 record.

Here’s everything you need to know about the MPL PH Season 12 playoffs.

MPL PH Season 12 playoffs format

The playoffs will feature a hybrid elimination format.

Blacklist International, Smart Omega, ONIC PH, and RSG PH, will start off in the play-ins stage.

The next round will adopt a double-elimination format where all matches are played in best-of-fives.

ECHO and AP Bren, who finished first and second in the regular season, respectively, have been granted a bye and will start directly in the upper bracket semifinal.

The playoffs will culminate in a grand final, which will be a best-of-seven series.

MPL PH Season 12 playoffs schedule

October 25 (Wednesday)

Upper bracket quarterfinal

ONIC PH and Smart Omega have been eliminated.

October 26 (Thursday)

Upper bracket semifinal

October 27 (Friday)

Upper bracket final

AP Bren qualifies for the M5 World Championship.

Lower bracket semifinal

RSG PH has been eliminated.

October 28 (Saturday)

Lower bracket final

ECHO has been eliminated.

October 29 (Sunday)

Grand final

AP Bren wins MPL Philippines Season 12.

Where to watch the MPL PH Season 12 playoffs?

All the games will be streamed live on Mobile Legends: Bang Bang’s official media platforms:

  • Facebook
  • YouTube
  • TikTok

Fans in the Philippines can watch the games live at EVM Convention Center in Quezon City, Philippines.
