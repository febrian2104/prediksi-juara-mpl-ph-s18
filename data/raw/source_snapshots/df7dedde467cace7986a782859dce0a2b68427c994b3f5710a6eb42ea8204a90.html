‘Sugal Din’: Coach Hito on TNC’s Rebuild for MPL PH Season 18

By Ramon Domingo - August 19, 2026

Will the Phoenix rise with this total rebuild of the team?

TNC Pro Team are changing things in a big way in MPL PH Season 18 and assistant coach Robert “Hito” Candoy explains that it was something that needed to happen. After the team’s tragic fall last season, he and head coach Patrick “E2MAX” Caidic looked at the tapes and came to the same conclusion.

“Kung titingnan mo yung season 17 namin, pagkatapos hinimay namin, parang hindi na nagco-connect yung mga players,” said Coach Hito to The GAME. “Malayo na ‘yung chemistry, nawala na. So nag-move on agad kami at nag-rebuild around [Justine “Zaida” Palma]. Tapos kami ni Coach E2MAX, nag-ready kami para sa mga rookies.”

It’s a bold move to keep your top-performing JunglerJunglerIn games such as Mobile Legends and League of Legends, a player who roams the neutral areas between lanes, collecting resources and ambushing opponents. Junglers set the pace of aGo to Jungler Glossary and have him be the spearhead of a brand new team. They’re also hoping to make a spark by bringing in rookies like Ervin “Vin” Franco into the fold. And with three rookies in their roster, TNC are taking a big risk this season but Coach Hito believes that they have to risk something either way.

“Both are risky naman. Like kung kumuha kami ng mga veterans, baka hindi sila mag-sync, baka may kanyang-kanyang perspective sa laro. With rookies, sugal din kung kaya nila mag-adapt. Pero na-try na namin ito with veterans so tinginan namin kung kaya namin with rookies.”

In the end, it’s up to the coaching staff to make sure that the team is ready for the MPL PH stage. They’ve had the entire off-season to build themselves up and Coach Hito believes that they have what they need to compete this season.

“May tiwala sila sa sarili nila, tiwala sa mga coaches, tapos tiwala kay God.”

Assistant coach Robert “Hito” Candoy said that after reviewing Season 17 footage, the coaching staff found the roster’s chemistry had broken down. TNC moved quickly to rebuild the team around jungler Justine “Zaida” Palma rather than retain the full previous lineup.

TNC is rebuilding around jungler Justine “Zaida” Palma, its top-performing player from the previous season. The team is pairing him with incoming rookies, including Ervin “Vin” Franco, rather than bringing in established veterans for MPL PH Season 18.

Coach Hito said both options carry risk — veterans may not sync or may bring conflicting playstyles, while rookies carry adaptation risk. Since TNC had already built a roster around veterans before, the coaching staff chose to try a rookie-based rebuild instead.

TNC Pro Team’s rebuilt roster includes three rookies, including Ervin “Vin” Franco, alongside returning jungler Justine “Zaida” Palma. The coaching staff, led by Robert “Hito” Candoy and head coach Patrick “E2MAX” Caidic, built the roster around this new core.

TNC Pro Team’s coaching staff is led by head coach Patrick “E2MAX” Caidic and assistant coach Robert “Hito” Candoy. Together they reviewed the team’s Season 17 performance and directed the roster rebuild ahead of MPL PH Season 18.
